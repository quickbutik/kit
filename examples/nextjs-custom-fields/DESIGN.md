# Custom Signs design brief

## What we sell

Custom aluminium, acrylic, wooden, banner, door plate, and round signs. Each product has three sizes and a free-layout artwork designer.

## Who buys

Customers ordering signage for business interiors, cafés, shops, and building entrances.

## How it should feel

"More restrained and professional." Keep the existing warm paper background and orange family, with quieter typography and consistent surfaces.

## Principles

- Show the sign clearly, with realistic installation examples.
- Keep the existing selection, designer, cart, and checkout flow.
- Use consistent radii, spacing, border colors, and interaction states.
- Label generated scenes as illustrative mockups.

## Decisions

- Home: existing split hero, material catalog, three scene examples, three-step explanation.
- Product: existing split layout, with product and scene views where a matching mockup exists.
- Designer: Design / Preview switch; live customer artwork composited over office, building entrance, or café backgrounds. Preserve artwork, selection, size, history, and zoom when switching. Label scene scale and lighting as approximate.
- Palette: warm paper, charcoal ink, restrained burnt-orange accent; shared tokens in globals.css.
- Type: existing Bricolage Grotesque display and Instrument Sans body; mono for dimensions and technical details. Keep the designer's font options.
- Imagery: clear acrylic in an office, aluminium beside a building entrance, birch plywood in a café. Locally stored and responsively served.
- Motion: short button press and hover feedback; respect reduced motion.

## Never

- Invent customer installations, testimonials, or certifications.
- Present homepage/product example artwork as the customer's own design. Designer Preview uses the actual current artwork.
- Add decorative floating animations or a new visual identity.
