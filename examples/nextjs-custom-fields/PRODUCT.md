# Custom Signs

## Register

brand

## Users

People ordering custom signage for offices, cafés, shops, and buildings. They need to compare materials and sizes, understand how a sign will look in a space, and create their own artwork.

## Product Purpose

A storefront for six sign materials, each available in three sizes, connected to a true-to-scale design canvas and Quickbutik checkout. Help customers choose a suitable sign and confidently move into designing it.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, professional, approachable. The user requested a small consistency and design improvement, with a more professional presentation and realistic scene mockups.

## Anti-references

Avoid a playful or decorative presentation, floating product animations, exaggerated effects, and a wholesale visual rebrand. Follow the existing warm palette without introducing unrelated brand references.

## Design Principles

- Improve the existing storefront incrementally.
- Show materials in believable settings as well as clear product views.
- Identify illustrative mockups as examples, distinct from the customer's artwork.
- Let customers preview their current artwork in a real setting directly from the designer, with approximate scene scale clearly indicated.
- Keep prices, sizes, and the path into the designer easy to find.
- Use shared visual treatments across the catalog, product pages, and navigation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No additional requirements were specified. Apply standard accessibility practices: readable contrast, descriptive image text, visible keyboard focus, labeled controls, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.
